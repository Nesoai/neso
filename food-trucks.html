Questions

I move around. Can the site keep up?

Yes. Tell us your regular spots and schedule on the form. When your schedule changes, send us the new one and we'll update the page.

I already have Instagram. Why a website?

Search engines and maps show websites first, and not everyone uses Instagram. A website gives people who search your name your spot, hours and menu without an app, and it links back to your Instagram.

Can people book catering through it?

Yes. The site can carry a catering and events request form that comes straight to you.

What if I don't like it?

We build the website before you pay so you can view it first. If it isn't right for you, you don't have to buy.
