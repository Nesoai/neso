How it is done today, honestly

This one is new and there is no crawler behind it. A principal checks the planning and zoning agendas for your jurisdictions every week, by hand, and reads them against the parcels you named. That is a real person's time, which is why it is capped at twenty-five members to start. A report is a snapshot of a day; this is the part that moves after the report is filed.

How it works

  • You name up to three parcels, or a jurisdiction and an area within it
  • We record what you are protecting or watching for
  • Every week we read the agendas and filings against that
  • You get a note the same day each week — including the weeks when nothing happened

Who it is for

  • Owners holding land through an entitlement cycle
  • Buyers under contract who need to know what changes before closing
  • Neighbours and community groups watching an adjacent site
  • Anyone who bought a Bird's Eye report and needs the picture kept current

When it is the wrong thing

  • You want the underlying zoning read — that is a Bird's Eye report first
  • You need same-day alerts; this is weekly
  • The jurisdiction publishes nothing online and takes a phone call — we will tell you before you pay
