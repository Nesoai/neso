At scope confirmation, never at checkout. You pay, we request three inputs — parcel or address, jurisdiction, and the decision being made — and the promised date is set when the last one lands.

Then the report says so, and says what it would take to get one. A screen that manufactures certainty it does not have is worse than no screen.
