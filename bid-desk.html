What it is not
It does not write your bid. That is the Bid Proposal service, from 1 credit ($50).
It does not score whether you will win. A fit-scored shortlist with reasoning is Procurement & Bid Sourcing.
It does not warrant a listing is still open. Agencies amend and cancel; the issuing portal governs and every item links to it.
A quiet week is reported as a quiet week. We do not pad a list to make it look busy.
