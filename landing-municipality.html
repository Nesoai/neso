Straight answers

How does procurement work with credits?

A credit block is a purchase like any other, and every deliverable is quoted against it in writing first. If your process needs a formal quote or a W-9 before a PO can be raised, ask and we will send both.

Who actually does the work?

A principal reviews and releases every deliverable — nothing ships without that. It is in the operating agreement, not just in practice.

What if the scope changes mid-way?

The quote is re-agreed. Turnaround pauses day-for-day while we are waiting on you, and a pause is never counted as a penalty day.
