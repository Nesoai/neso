Straight answers

The things people ask before they trust any of it.

What does "free" actually include?

The complete analysis, on your own numbers — every section, the real figures, the full visual. No account, no email wall, no trial that expires. Paying adds the printable exhibit, the editing and the depth. It never adds the insight. If a stranger would not call the free result finished, the paid version does not deserve to sell.

How does NESO charge?

Three ways, and they are not interchangeable. Tools are free with a one-time unlock that is owned forever. Research on a named parcel is a fixed price. Advisory work is quoted in credits at a published rate before anything starts. What each one costs is on the site, next to the thing it prices — deliberately not restated here, because a price with two homes eventually disagrees with itself.

When does the clock start?

At scope confirmation, never at checkout. You pay, we ask for three things — the parcel or address, the jurisdiction, and the decision you are trying to make — and the promised date is set when the last one lands. If we are waiting on you, the clock pauses day for day, and a pause is never counted as a penalty day.

Who actually looks at the work?

Charles reviews and releases every real-estate deliverable NESO produces. That is in the operating agreement, not just in practice. It caps how much NESO can take on, and the cap is the point — a buyer is paying for somebody senior having looked.

Can you turn it around faster?

Rush halves the turnaround with a one-day floor, capped at two slots per week firm-wide. When the week is full the option is refused rather than queued. That cap is what makes the promise real.

How new is NESO?

New. The entity, the bank account, the tools, the reports and the machinery underneath them are built and live; what does not exist yet is years of closed engagements to point at, and saying otherwise would be the fastest way to lose anyone worth talking to. What is behind the work is seventeen years of somebody else's — Charles has been doing this on projects and inside agencies since long before NESO.

What if none of the six sound like me?

Say what decision you are trying to make rather than which product you want. A principal reads every enquiry and replies within a business day. If the honest answer is a free tool, that is the answer you will get — which is why the tools are free in the first place.
