Four questions

The ones people ask first.

What does free really mean?

You get the whole answer, using your own numbers. No account, no email, and nothing runs out. Paying adds the printed copy and more detail. It never adds the answer.

What does it cost?

The tools are free. A land report has one price. Other work is quoted in writing before it starts. Every price is on the website, next to the thing it belongs to.

Who checks the work?

Charles reads and approves every land report before it leaves. That is a rule NESO wrote down, not just a habit.

Where does NESO work?

Anywhere in the United States. The tools have no geography in them at all — they run on your numbers wherever you are. The name points at where the two of us know the ground well: the Northeast — New England and New York — and the Southern states. On a land report, where the parcel is changes how much of the answer comes from the record and how much comes from knowing the place. We say which is which in the report, and we say it before you pay.
