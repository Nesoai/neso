Straight answers

Do you submit the bid for me?

No, and that is deliberate. You submit it. The submission carries your certifications and your signature, and the portal account is yours. We hand you the finished response and you file it.

What if I have almost nothing prepared?

Then Assembly is the wrong tier, because Assembly only formats what you send. Ask for Researched or Authored and say plainly what you do not have. We will tell you which one fits before you pay for either.

How fast can you turn one around?

Assembly is two to three business days from scope approval. Rush halves that with a floor of one day, and we hold only two rush slots a week across the whole firm — when the week is full we say no rather than take it and slip.

What are credits, and do they expire?

Credits are how our services are priced: one credit is $50, published, the same for everyone. You can buy a single credit or a block of 10, 20, 40 or 80, with a published discount of 0, 4, 8 or 12 percent. Blocks are good for thirteen weeks from the day you buy them, or thirty-nine weeks on the $10 a month Rollover membership. We contact you before a block lapses. Anything unused goes to our community fund rather than back into our revenue.

Can you guarantee we win it?

No. Nobody honestly can. What we can do is make sure the response answers what was asked, in the format that was asked for, by the date it was due — which is where a surprising number of bids are lost before anyone reads the price.

Do you work on bids I am competing for against another client?

Ask us before you send anything. If we are already working the same solicitation for someone else we will tell you and decline, rather than take both.
