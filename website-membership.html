Why this exists

Most quotes for a small-business website start at a thousand dollars.

That price is real, and for a big build it is fair. It is also why so many good businesses are still running on a phone number and a page they do not control. This membership is the other answer: a straightforward site, built properly, for the price of a couple of lunches a month — because a business with twelve customers a week still deserves to be findable.

You are not renting a template

A person reads your form, writes your words, and builds your pages. A principal looks at it before it goes live. Nothing publishes automatically.

It stays up

Hosting is included and checked every day by the same system that watches NESO's own sites. If yours goes down, we find out before you do.

You own your name

Your domain is yours. If you ever leave, it transfers to you at no charge. We will put that in writing before you pay anything.

How it goes

Four steps, and three of them are ours

You fill in the brief

Fifteen minutes, on your phone. What you do, what you charge for, where you work, your hours, and how people reach you. If a question does not apply, say so — that is an answer.

We build it and show you

You get a link to look at before anything is public. The clock starts the moment your form is in — three business days — and if you filled the form in before joining, it starts when you join.

You ask for one round of changes

Wording, photos, hours, prices, contact details. One revision is included and it is a real revision, not a typo fix.

It goes live and we keep it current

New hours, a new phone number, a price change, a seasonal note. Send it over. Small changes are what the monthly fee is for.

Your name is yours. The catch is that there isn't one.

Registered to you, not to us. Your text, your photos, your prices and every enquiry that comes in are yours. If you leave, the domain goes with you the same week, at no charge, and we will not hold it for a day.

The site we build is ours, licensed to you

The same arrangement every website platform runs on. While you are a member it is yours to use, we keep it working, and you never think about it again.

Do I have to have a domain already?

No. Tell us on the form and we will register one in your name for $39, which covers the first year. If you already own one and cannot remember where, say so — finding it is part of the job.

What if I already have a website I hate?

Then you are the easiest kind of client, because you already know what is wrong with it. We build a new one and point your existing address at it. Your old site stays untouched until you say go live.

How long does it take?

Three business days from the moment you send the form to a site you can look at, and two more after you send your revision notes. What happens if I cancel?

After the three-month minimum you can cancel any month. The site comes down, your domain transfers to you at no cost, and your content is yours. There is no exit fee and nothing is held hostage. If you want to keep the site itself, that is the $100 handover — the files, yours to take anywhere. Refunds follow the published policy: 14 days, closing when your site goes live, which is the point where the work has actually been done.

Why a three-month minimum?

Because the whole build happens before your second payment. One month would mean building a site for twenty dollars, and a price that only works if people leave immediately is not a price, it is a trick. Three months is the honest version. If you would rather not commit monthly, pay $200 for the year as a founding member — ten months for twelve, and that rate is yours on every renewal after it.

Why is it this cheap?

Because we built the machinery for our own eight products first, and the same intake, review and hosting layer runs your site at nearly no extra cost to us.
