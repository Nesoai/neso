Straight answers

Who owns it?

Your web address is in your name. Your words and photos are yours. If you leave, the address goes with you the same week, free. The site we build stays ours and you use it while you are a member — the same way Wix or Squarespace works. If you want to keep the site itself, it is $100 and the files are yours to take anywhere.

Why three months to start?

We build the whole site before your second payment. One month would mean building a website for twenty dollars. After three months you can stop any time you like.

What will mine actually look like?

Like one of the eight examples — your words, your colours, your pictures. We do not hand you a template and a login; a person builds it and a principal looks at it before it goes live.

What is the logo like?

We design three and you pick one. They are simple and clean — your name, set well, with a mark if it suits you. You can have the colour changed, and that is the extent of it.

It is a logo, not a brand. If you want colours, fonts and a whole look worked out with you over several rounds, that is real design work and we would quote it separately. And we do not check whether a name or mark is free to use — that is a trademark question, and it is yours to ask.

Do you do SEO?

The basics, yes, and they are included. Your business name, your town, what you do and your hours go where Google reads them, your pages are named properly, and we check your site against your Google listing so the two agree. That is the part that matters most for a local business, and it is done once, properly, at build.

What we will not do is promise a position. Nobody can, and anybody who does is guessing. Ongoing SEO — someone working on rankings month after month — is a different job at a different price. Ask and we will tell you honestly whether it is worth it for your kind of business.

What if I already have a website?

Then you already know what is wrong with it, which makes this easy. We build the new one and point your address at it when you say go. Your old site stays up until then.

How fast, really?

Three business days from the minute you send the form. Not from a phone call. Your round of changes takes two more days after you send them. If we are ever going to be slower, you hear it before you pay.

Who are you?

Two people. Mallory builds and runs the software. The other has spent seventeen years in planning and land use, and checks work before it goes out. There is no account manager. You will be talking to one of us.
