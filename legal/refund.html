Refund Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

One principle governs every window below: a refund is available until the thing you paid for becomes irreversible. For a software unlock that moment is redemption. For research it is the moment we confirm your engagement and senior work begins. For a call it is the call. Each window has a day count and an event; whichever comes first closes it.

What this is not

It is not a satisfaction guarantee on delivered work. A released report or deliverable has been researched, reviewed by a principal, and sent; one revision is included with every report, and that revision — not a refund — is the remedy for something we got wrong. If we miss a promised date through our own fault, that is a different conversation, and we will have it openly.

Mechanics

  • Purchases are made through Gumroad, which processes refunds to the original payment method. The window shown on each listing matches this schedule; if a listing ever disagrees with this page, this page governs and we will honour the longer of the two for your purchase.
  • A refunded unlock has its code deactivated. A refunded purchase reverses any referral commission attached to it.
  • To request a refund, reply to your Gumroad receipt or email hello@nesohq.com with the receipt reference. We respond the same business day.

The clock on reports, plainly

Paying does not start your turnaround. We ask for three things — the parcel or address, the jurisdiction, and the decision you are trying to make — and the promised date is set when the last one arrives. That is also the moment your refund window can close early: it is when our work genuinely starts.
