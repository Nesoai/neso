Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

NESO ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us — name, email, phone, and business or project details you submit through our forms or by email.
  • Automatic data — basic device and usage information collected through cookies and analytics as you browse.

How we use it

To respond to your inquiry, prepare quotes, deliver services, improve the site, and meet legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (for example, our form and analytics providers) or when required by law. Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are excluded from this sharing: they are never shared or sold to third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. See Text messages below.

Text messages (SMS)

If you give us a mobile number on one of our forms, or if you text our business number (901) 295-0322, you agree that a NESO principal may reply by text about your inquiry. Our messages are conversational and written by a person. We do not send marketing blasts, and we do not use purchased or rented contact lists.

  • Mobile numbers are not shared or sold. Mobile information and SMS consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. A number is disclosed only to the messaging provider that transmits the message for us, and only in order to deliver it.
  • Message frequency varies by conversation, and is typically fewer than 10 messages per month.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • To stop messages, reply STOP at any time. For help, reply HELP or email hello@nesohq.com.
  • Opting out of text messages does not affect anything else. We will continue by email if you prefer, and it never affects work already underway.

Cookies & analytics

We use cookies and analytics to understand site usage. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Retention

We keep information as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (for example, under GDPR or CCPA).

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
