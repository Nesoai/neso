What membership is, plainly

  • Up to five areas. You name where you want work — cities, counties, or a radius. We match against those and nothing else.
  • Opportunities when they exist, not on a schedule. We forward what genuinely matches. Some weeks that is several; some weeks it is none. We do not pad the list to look busy, and we do not guarantee volume.
  • Introductions, not exclusivity. More than one member may cover an area and a trade. When a contact asks, we send the members who fit; the work goes to whoever wins it.
  • Your pricing stays yours. The pricing form exists so we represent you accurately — we never quote for you, negotiate for you, or take a cut of the job.

The honest limits

This is a new line and you would be among its first members — which is exactly why the founding price exists. The bid database is real and running today; the agent-and-owner network is real and growing. What does not exist yet is a track record of placed work, and we will not invent one. What you are buying at $11 is a working search and a seat at the front of the list as this builds.
