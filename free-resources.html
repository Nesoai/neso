Funding and planning

Scores seven funding channels (federal and state programs, foundations, SBA and CDFI lending, and more) against a business and shows where the odds are real.

Opengrantfit.nesohq.com

Turns pricing, costs and growth into a monthly model with break-even and runway: the financial section of a plan.

MarketFit

Sizes the local market for a first location, a new location or an expansion, and gives an honest read on whether the slice pencils.
